Enhancing Information Assurance Practices

A client-based security and risk-management case study for Kyro Core I.T. Solutions, focused on improving data protection, operational resilience, and practical cybersecurity controls for a local IT service provider.

Organization context

Kyro Core I.T. Solutions is a small Dagupan City technology and security service provider offering CCTV sales and installation, web development and maintenance, SEO, and graphic design and printing services.

Purpose

The project identifies threats and vulnerabilities, recommends appropriate security controls, and establishes policies that strengthen cybersecurity, data protection, risk management, and business continuity.

Identified risks

Access & identity

Weak or reused passwords, phishing, and poor authentication practices can enable unauthorized access.

Systems & privacy

Unauthorized CCTV access and unpatched websites can expose sensitive footage, client data, or web systems.

Continuity

Data loss and the absence of a formal backup plan can interrupt operations and damage client trust.

Security recommendations

  • Enforce strong passwords and multi-factor authentication.
  • Encrypt and regularly update CCTV infrastructure.
  • Perform vulnerability assessments and apply patches promptly.
  • Use onsite and cloud backups with a disaster-recovery plan.
  • Train employees to recognize phishing and practice cyber hygiene.

Policies & governance

  • Acceptable Use Policy
  • Data Privacy Policy
  • Incident Response Policy
  • Access Control Policy based on least privilege
  • Formal management authorization and risk acceptance

Monitoring and continuity

Information assurance is treated as an ongoing process. The report recommends monthly reviews, emerging-threat monitoring, effectiveness checks, post-incident reviews, log analysis, policy updates, and periodic risk reassessment so controls remain aligned with changing operations and threats.

My contribution

As Technical Researcher, I focused on technical security solutions and tools applicable to the organization and supported the group’s risk, control, and continuity recommendations.

What I learned

This project strengthened my ability to connect risk identification with actionable controls, communicate security priorities clearly, and think about protection as a balance of people, policy, technology, and recovery.
